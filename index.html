04 — About

About AmoebaX

AmoebaX is an independent research and engineering effort focused on infrastructure, execution systems, artificial intelligence, and open-source software. The name reflects the philosophy: organisms that survive by adapting, not by being rigid.

Everything built here is designed to be observable, composable, and open. The work spans from low-level execution primitives to language model research — connected by a shared belief that the best software learns from its environment.
