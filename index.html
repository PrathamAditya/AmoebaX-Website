04 / About AmoebaX

Independent Research & Engineering

AmoebaX began as an exploration into adaptive systems—software that doesn't just execute predefined instructions, but learns from its environment and optimizes its own execution paths.

We are an independent research and engineering collective focused on the intersection of low-level systems programming, distributed architectures, and applied machine learning. Our goal is to build open-source primitives that enable more resilient and self-optimizing infrastructure.

By combining the safety and performance of systems languages with the dynamic capabilities of modern AI, we are laying the groundwork for the next generation of computing infrastructure.

Let's build something that evolves.
